Why this deserves its own investor surface

Reporting repair

A restored SEC-current status is meaningful, but it is easy to miss when presented as one press release among many.

Capital-markets intent

The strategic-review and potential-relisting narrative should sit beside current reporting status and governance—not in separate archive entries.

Operating evidence

The Permian updates give the story an operational backbone, preventing the investor page from reading like a purely financial-engineering exercise.
